# Brand Voice & Positioning Guide: EVBikes Pakistan (PakEV)

## 1. Brand Identity & Core Value Proposition
- **Product Name:** EVBikes Pakistan (`PakEV`)
- **Brand Purpose:** Cut through deceptive marketing in Pakistan's electric bike market. Provide 100% transparent, mathematical, and field-tested side-by-side vehicle comparisons.
- **Tagline:** *Asli Range, Sahi Price, PAVE Phase 2 Subsidy Ka Mukammal Hisab.*

---

## 2. Target Audience Personas

### Persona A: "The Street-Smart Commuter" (Tariq, 29, Karachi)
- **Profile:** Rides a 2018 Honda CD70 from Gulshan-e-Iqbal to I.I. Chundrigar Road daily (42 km round-trip).
- **Pain Points:** Spends Rs 9,500/month on petrol, frequent oil changes and tuning. Wants an electric bike but fears getting stranded midway or paying Rs 60,000 for battery replacement after 1 year.
- **Tone to use:** Direct, numbers-backed, relatable Roman Urdu + English technical terms. Speak in savings: *"Mahana Rs 7,500 ki seedhi bachat."*

### Persona B: "The First-Time Student Buyer" (Zain, 21, Lahore)
- **Profile:** University student wanting an electric scooter (Yadea, Evee, Metro) under the PAVE Phase 2 scheme.
- **Pain Points:** Confused about whether balloting still exists or if it's first-come-first-served. Doesn't know the difference between Graphene and Lithium batteries.
- **Tone to use:** Clear, advisory, step-by-step guidance on pave.gov.pk application.

---

## 3. Brand Voice & Communication Principles

| Principle | What We Do | What We NEVER Do |
|---|---|---|
| **Radical Transparency** | Tell the unvarnished real-world range: *"Company claims 100 km, but on Karachi roads with a pillion rider expect 65 km."* | Never repeat inflated manufacturer brochure claims without testing. |
| **Battery Truth** | Call Graphene what it is: heavy lead-acid with 1.5–2 year life. Explain why Lithium LFP costs more upfront but lasts 5 years. | Never call Graphene "super-battery" or hide replacement costs. |
| **Actionable Math** | Compare petrol costs vs electricity units: *"1 Full Charge costs Rs 55 on your home meter."* | Never make vague claims like "runs on pennies". |
| **Automotive Authority** | Use crisp automotive terminology: Torque, kW rating, controller current, regenerative braking, IP67 water ingress. | Never sound like an uninformed blog aggregate. |

---

## 4. Key Messaging Anchors
- **Primary Hook:** *Har EV Bike Ka Aamne-Samne Muqabla.*
- **PAVE Subsidy Hook:** *Government Ki Rs. 80,000 Subsidy Ke Baad Asli Net Price Kya Hai?*
- **Savings Hook:** *Honda 70 vs Electric Bike: 18 Mahine Mein Puri Bike Free!*
